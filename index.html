    1. 1

      Ask it

      Think of a yes-or-no question. Say it out loud for extra drama.

    2. 2

      Squish it

      Give it a good squeeze. The colorful balls inside shuffle around.

    3. 3

      See your fortune

      The color that pops to the front is your answer. Check the key on the box.
